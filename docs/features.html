ReefTracker Feature Overview

Last updated: 19 August 2026 · covers published version 1.3.0

ReefTracker is an offline-first reef-aquarium companion for Android and iOS. Log your water tests in seconds and see at a glance whether every value is healthy – with color-coded safe ranges, history graphs, trends and early warnings, dosing tools, maintenance reminders, and safe backups. Everything is stored on your device unless you choose your own cloud backup: no account, no cloud requirement, no ads.

Each feature below is either included for everyone (STANDARD) or part of the ReefTracker Pro tier (PRO). Features marked EXPERIMENTAL stay hidden until you switch them on in Settings → Experimental.

Founder's Edition: the Pro tier hasn't launched yet – install ReefTracker now and every Pro feature on this page stays free for you forever, even on a new device.

Measurement import & meters

FeatureEdition
Hanna Lab file import – import the test history CSV shared from the Hanna Lab app (HI97115 Marine Master): original timestamps, grouped test sessions, smart de-duplication so re-imports only add what's new, and a one-tap undoPRO
Hanna checker direct connection EXPERIMENTAL – run measurements live over Bluetooth on a Hanna HI97115C photometer, method by method or via saved test sets, and save the confirmed results with the meter's timestamps. Before saving you can untick any value you don't trust, or send it back to the meter to be measured again – the new reading replaces it and shows what it replaced. Built-in reagent reaction timers (15 s / 30 s / 2 min) beep when the wait is over, the app beeps again as each result arrives, and the screen stays awake while a test is running. Once connected, the checker also gets its own card on the Connected devices page – name, serial number, when it last measured, and a button that starts the next measurement right there. Measurements only: meter settings and firmware updates stay in Hanna's own Hanna Lab appPRO
Environment capture on live measurements EXPERIMENTAL – when saving a Hanna checker session, also record the aquarium's current salinity, temperature, pH and ORP from the connected meters assigned to it (ReefFactory meters and Red Sea ReefControl), all in the same session. Opt-in and remembered; any value can be excluded with a tap, values the checker itself measured are never duplicated, and when several devices report the same parameter the first one in your chosen brand and device order winsPRO
Checker display scan EXPERIMENTAL – point the camera at a Hanna pocket checker's LCD and save the shown value without typing; 14 checker models supported, recognition runs fully on-device (no photos stored or uploaded)PRO
Automatic checker recognition – the scanned checker's model is identified from its case color and the reading's display format, so scanning is truly one-tap; the app only asks when models would interpret the value differently, and never guesses silentlyPRO
Scan viewfinder with pinch-to-zoom (starts at 2×) – read the display from a comfortable distance instead of holding the phone against the checkerPRO
Experimental features are opt-in – the checker connection and display scan stay completely hidden until switched on in Settings → ExperimentalSTANDARD
Optional quick-scan camera button next to "Add reading" – one-tap scanning for checker owners, no lost screen space for everyone else (off by default)STANDARD
ReefFactory device live values EXPERIMENTAL – read the current salinity, pH and temperature from ReefFactory local meters (Salinity Guardian, pH Monitor, Temperature Controller) over your Wi-Fi and save them as measurements. Add a meter by its IP address or hostname and the app identifies the model for you, naming it after its ReefFactory product. Every meter is read when you open the dashboard; pull to refresh or use Refresh all to re-read them, save one card from its menu, or use Save all for the lot. The Temperature Controller's card also shows a Heating or Cooling badge whenever it is actually running that output, straight from the controller itself. Press and hold a card to move it into the order you prefer. Read-only: it only reads values – settings, calibration and firmware stay in the ReefFactory app. Works on your local network only: your phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi network as the meters, there is no cloud connectionPRO
Red Sea ReefBeat dosing-pump status EXPERIMENTAL – see your ReefDose pump (2- or 4-head) live over your Wi-Fi: each head's supplement, a gauge of today's dosing progress against the scheduled daily amount, and days of supplement remaining colored green, amber or red as the container runs low – plus warnings for missed doses, needed recalibration, a low backup battery or a wrong pump clock. The card's menu opens today's dosing queue – every dose the pump still has to deliver before midnight, with its time, supplement and volume, and the running total. Add the pump by its IP address or hostname and the app identifies it for you, then press and hold a card to move it into the order you prefer when several devices share the dashboard. Read-only: dosing, schedules and calibration stay in the ReefBeat app. Works on your local network only: your phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi network as the pump, there is no cloud connectionPRO
Red Sea ReefATO+ status EXPERIMENTAL – see your auto-top-off unit live on the same dashboard: water level, the sensor's temperature probe, today's top-offs (fills and volume), your tank's average daily evaporation, and how much reserve water is left – volume and days until empty, colored green, amber or red as the reservoir runs low. The leak-sensor row always tells you whether the sensor is disconnected, not enabled or standing by Dry, and a red warning names whether it found RO/DI or aquarium water when a leak is detected – alongside warnings for level-sensor trouble or the fill pump running. Read-only: settings and calibration stay in the ReefBeat app. Works on your local network only: your phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi network as the unit, there is no cloud connectionPRO
Red Sea ReefMat status EXPERIMENTAL – see your roller filter live on the same dashboard: how much fleece is left on the roll, as a gauge against the roll's full length with the days left at the current rate, colored green, amber or red as it runs out – plus how much the mat advanced today, its daily average, and the date you fitted the roll with its age in days. Warns you when the roll is spent or nearly spent, when the sensor needs cleaning, when automatic advancing is switched off, and while the mat is advancing. Read-only: settings and manual advancing stay in the ReefBeat app. Works on your local network only: your phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi network as the mat, there is no cloud connectionPRO
Red Sea ReefRun status EXPERIMENTAL – see your return and skimmer pumps live on the same dashboard: each pump's name, its speed as a circular gauge, and its motor temperature, with a green Sensor badge on any pump the overflow sensor controls. Warns when a pump or its water-level sensor isn't detected, when a pump reports a problem – a skimmer paused with a full collection cup says "Full cup" plainly – when the level sensor a pump depends on has gone offline, and about a low backup battery or a wrong clock. Read-only: speeds and schedules stay in the ReefBeat app. Works on your local network only: your phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi network as the controller, there is no cloud connectionPRO
Red Sea ReefLED status EXPERIMENTAL – see your light live on the same dashboard: current white, blue and moon output as gauges, the moon phase being simulated, fan speed and heatsink temperature. Warns you when the fixture has been knocked or hung at an angle, while an acclimation ramp is running (with the days left), and about a low backup battery or a wrong clock. Read-only: programs and intensities stay in the ReefBeat app. Works on your local network only: your phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi network as the light, there is no cloud connectionPRO
Red Sea ReefWave status EXPERIMENTAL – see all your wave pumps on the dashboard in one card, one line each: the flow they're set to run right now, shown as a circular gauge that follows their own schedule through the day, next to the pump's name. Salinity follows your selected ppt or SG unit, ORP is shown in mV, and the temperature built into each combined probe gets its own clearly labelled row in your selected °C or °F. If a leak detector is attached, the card also shows green Dry or a red Leak detected warning. Save the controller's salinity, pH, ORP and first available probe temperature from its menu or with Save all; the same current values can be proposed alongside a Hanna checker session. Value colors follow the ranges configured for your aquarium in ReefTracker, just like the Wall display, where each attached primary water parameter and the first available probe temperature gets its own large card and 24-hour line. Neptune Apex probe values EXPERIMENTAL – read your Apex over your Wi-Fi and save its probe values as measurements: temperature, pH, ORP and salinity, plus a Trident’s alkalinity, calcium and magnesium alongside them. No hunting for IP addresses in your router's settings. Devices the app can't read yet are still listed, so you always know what was found, and typing an IP address by hand remains available for unusual network setupsPRO
Automatic address repair EXPERIMENTAL – devices are recognised by their hardware identity, not their address, so when your router hands a device a new IP and it stops responding, a scan spots it and one tap points the app back at it – keeping the device's name, aquarium and position on the dashboardPRO
Rename any device EXPERIMENTAL – the Edit item in a card's menu renames the meter or ReefBeat device, so the dashboard calls it what you call it. The card carries that name alone: the model code and IP address the app needs are no longer printed under itPRO
All your devices on one page EXPERIMENTAL – every meter, pump, filter, light and controller you've connected lives on a single Connected devices page with its own tab in the bottom bar. A row of brand buttons across the top scopes it – tap All to see the lot grouped by brand, or one brand to see only its devices. Brands you don't own never appear, and with a single brand the buttons disappear entirely. Swipe left or right anywhere on the page to move to the next or previous brand – no scrolling back up to the buttons, and the page returns to the top so you can see where you landed. Each device keeps its own full card – nothing is summarised away. Refresh all and Save all float within thumb's reach however far the list is scrolled, and adding a device sits in the top barSTANDARD
Refresh and save only what you're looking at EXPERIMENTAL – pull down past the top or tap Refresh to read exactly the devices currently on screen, so filtering to one brand doesn't wake the rest of your equipment. Save all writes the readings of every meter in view in one go, and merges values taken moments apart into a single measurement instead of several. Its count tells you how many distinct parameters will be saved, without counting duplicate probe readings. It only appears when the devices in view can actually measure somethingPRO
Choose which brand wins EXPERIMENTAL – when two devices report the same reading for the same aquarium, the one higher up the page is the one that gets saved, and the confirmation tells you which device it came from rather than picking silently. Reorder brands from the button beside them, or press and hold a card and move it above its neighbours, and you've set that preference – there's no separate setting to hunt forPRO
Wrong-looking device readings are questioned, not stored EXPERIMENTAL – before a reading from a connected device joins your history the app checks it, the same way it checks what you type in by hand. A value far outside the typical range for that parameter, or one that reads as nothing at all – 0 dKH, no salt in the water, the classic sign a probe has come loose – is shown to you with its typical range beside it. Keep it if you know it's real, or skip just that value and still save everything else the other devices reportedPRO
Connected devices list – see what you own even without a Pro entitlement: the Connected devices page always lists your hardware with its brand, model, address and when the app last reached it (in each card's Details), whether or not live reading is unlockedSTANDARD
Wall display mode – turn a wall-mounted tablet into an always-on board of your aquarium: big color-coded value cards readable across the room, one per device and parameter (plus cards fed by your own measurements for everything else), each with a live 24-hour graph built from the mode's own polling – a min/max band shows overnight swings, and your hand tests appear as markers on the same line. Status tiles show the ATO water level, leak sensor and reservoir – the days it lasts turning amber, then red, as it runs low – every dosing head's remaining supplement – green, amber or red as the container runs low, in days or months – the fleece roll and the skimmer cup, and a bottom strip lists today's due tasks. The board never scrolls: tiles size themselves to the screen and extra pages rotate through automatically. It keeps the screen awake, hides the system bars, dims itself at night (a tap wakes it for a minute), and gently shifts the layout against OLED burn-in. Works with zero devices too – your stored readings still make a useful boardPRO
Wall display cards, your way – every value every device reports starts on the board; hide the duplicates you don't want and drag the rest into your own order. Hide all of a device's cards and the wall stops contacting that device entirely – and an app update can never quietly turn it back onPRO
Wall display auto-start – flip one switch and the app boots straight into the board, so a rebooted tablet finds its way back to the wall by itself; leaving the mode takes a deliberate press-and-hold, so a brushed-past panel stays putPRO
